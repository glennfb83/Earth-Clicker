🌍 Welcome to Earth Clicker! 🌍

Save the world one click at a time

🎯 Your Goal

Earth is in crisis! Your mission is to heal our planet by accumulating 1,000,000 Impact Points. Through your efforts, you'll watch Earth transform from a dystopian wasteland back to a thriving paradise.

🕹️ How to Play

🌍 Click the Earth

Click on the Earth image to earn 1 Impact Point per click. This is your primary method of generating points.

🌳 Plant Trees (+1/click)

Spend 10 Impact Points to permanently increase your click power by 1. Trees are essential for restoring the ecosystem!

⚡ Build Solar Panels (+1/sec)

Spend 25 Impact Points to gain 1 automatic point per second. Renewable energy works while you're away!

📊 The 3 Phases

🌑 Phase 1: Dystopian Earth (0 - 100k points)

Earth is dark and dying. Focus on clicking and planting trees to begin the healing process.

🌤️ Phase 2: Earth Recovering (100k - 500k points)

The planet starts showing signs of life. Upgrade your solar panels to harness clean energy!

🌈 Phase 3: Thriving Earth (500k - 1M points)

Earth is coming back to life! Continue pushing to reach 1 million points for victory!

💡 Pro Tip: Upgrade costs increase each time you buy them, so balance your spending between click power and passive income. Your progress is automatically saved!
